Translate a YouTube video

Paste a link, pick a target language, and get a dubbed MP4 plus translated subtitles. Processing runs on your machine (this app). Install ffmpeg and yt-dlp on PATH.

This is a local alternative to hosted “translate & dub” services: it uses YouTube captions or Whisper, Google Translate, Microsoft Edge neural TTS, and ffmpeg. It does not include lip-sync or speaker voice cloning.
